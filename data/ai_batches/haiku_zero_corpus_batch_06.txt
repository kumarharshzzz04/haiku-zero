# HAIKU ZERO — Batch 06: Carefully Drafted Original Poems
# Meter not independently verified; inspect before training.

Rain beads on the rail
A sparrow shakes its wet wings
The bus sighs away

The pond wears thin ice
A reed bends under the snow
Tracks stop at the bank

Your note in the book
A corner folded in blue
The lamp burns past ten

The bean vine climbs up
Its tendrils catch the old wire
Bees work in the heat

The tide leaves a pool
A small crab tests the bright sand
Clouds cover the sun

A train slows to a stop
One suitcase waits by the bench
The doors close again

The porch light flickers
A moth circles the warm glass
Stars fill the dark field

Steam clouds the glass
The knife rests beside a plum
Rain taps at the sill

Leaves gather by the gate
A wheelbarrow leans on the shed
The crows cross at dusk

The river bends south
A kingfisher skims the surface
Ripples reach the reeds

Fog wraps round the pass
A bell sounds from far below
The trail disappears

A shop sign buzzes
Rain blurs the painted letters
The lock turns at dusk

Dust floats in sunlight
A pressed fern marks the page
Footsteps fade upstairs

The gate hangs askew
A horse noses the latch loose
Wind moves through the wheat

The buoy bell rings
Fog softens the edge of shore
Boats wait for dawn

Chalk dust on the desk
A paper plane glides and falls
The bell ends the day

Warm glass holds the rain
A fern unfurls by the door
Drops shine on each frond

The curtain falls down
One seat remains in the light
The stagehands sweep dust

The chisel slips once
A curl of cedar hits the floor
Sun warms the shavings

The well mirrors stars
A lizard slips under stone
Heat leaves the red sand

The engine grows quiet
Mist closes around the pier
One bell answers back

The stalls fold away
A peach rolls under the cart
Rain cleans the street

The ladder leans back
A pear drops into the grass
Wasps circle the bruise

The iron cables hum
A bicycle crosses at dusk
The river keeps pace

Flour dusts the sleeve
The baker lifts the oven door
Warm bread fills the street

Coins lie underwater
A leaf turns above each face
The pump starts again

The nets dry on stone
Silver scales cling to the twine
Boats rock at noon

Snow rests on the mail slot
A red mitten lies nearby
No one claims it yet

Plum blossoms open
Petals settle on the roof tiles
A sparrow shakes them

Cicadas fill noon
The pond holds a broken moon
Night cools the stone steps

The hinge gives a cry
A stripe of sun crosses the floor
Dust turns in the beam

A jar of brown beans
Each label names a lost field
Cold shelves hold spring

The barge turns slowly
A willow trails fingers in brown water
The lock bell rings

The ladder rests in grass
The farmer checks each empty branch
Clouds darken the west

The sun slips behind dunes
A beetle crosses the warm track
Night cools the stone

A crow lifts one wing
The branch sways after it leaves
Apples bruise below

The dome turns west
A star appears through the slit
The town sleeps below
